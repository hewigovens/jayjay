# Japanese glossary

There is no official Japanese jj documentation. jj concepts follow the terms Japanese jj articles use; Git and GitHub concepts follow [GitHub Docs (ja)](https://docs.github.com/ja), including its style: katakana words in a compound are separated by a space (コミット メッセージ), and loanwords keep the trailing ー (エディター, フォルダー).
