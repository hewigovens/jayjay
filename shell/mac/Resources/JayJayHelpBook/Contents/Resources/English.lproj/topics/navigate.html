History

Navigate history

The graph shows jj changes as a DAG. Click a change to inspect it, or use j/k, arrow keys, and Ctrl+N/Ctrl+P to move through rows.

  • Use revset chips to narrow the graph to mine, bookmarks, conflicts, trunk, or all changes.
  • Type a custom revset when the presets are too broad.
  • Drag a change row to preview and confirm a rebase.
  • Drag a bookmark chip to move the bookmark, or drag the working-copy @ chip to edit another change.

The DAG view is built around jj changes, bookmarks, conflicts, divergent changes, and revsets.
