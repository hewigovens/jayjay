Diff fixes

  • Selection clamps to end-of-line. Highlighting no longer trails into empty space past a line's last glyph, and multi-line selections that cross a short→long line transition now highlight every line in full instead of stopping at the previous line's width.

DAG polish

  • Lane connectors now draw short stubs through each node, so the graph reads as one continuous line through commits instead of breaking at every row.

Internal

  • Editor and terminal launch logic moved to jayjay-core (Rust) so the SwiftUI app and the in-progress GPUI shell share one implementation. No behavior change.
