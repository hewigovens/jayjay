Repo Overview

  • Lane cards are easier to scan: workspaces and bookmarks sit on their own row and collapse into a +N chip when they don't fit, and the last line shows the change count and age, or what needs attention.
  • The label on trunk names each lane's base once for the whole group, with the base commit and its age beneath, instead of repeating it on every card.
  • Click a lane to open its panel: the base, a Rebase onto trunk button when it is behind, what needs attention, its workspaces with an Open button, its bookmarks, and every change with its full title. Press Esc to step back from a change to its lane and then close the panel.
  • A change's title, ids, and description can be selected and copied.
