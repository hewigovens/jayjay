- In **Edit Diff**, choose **Commit Selected** to commit the checked working-copy lines and leave the remaining edits in place. If the working copy moves before you apply, reopen it to make a fresh selection.
- With whitespace ignored, whitespace-only edits in the surrounding context stay out of the selected change.
- Fold individual files in stacked diffs. Large diffs and unavailable previews occupy one compact row.
- The description editor opens larger. Drag its corner to make room for a longer message.
