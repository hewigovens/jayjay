### New

- Repo Overview comes to Linux: Repository > Repo Overview (Ctrl+Shift+O), the command palette, or the Overview button in the workspace picker opens every lane of mutable work as a tree growing from trunk. Lane cards show their workspaces and bookmarks and what needs attention; clicking a lane opens its base, a Rebase onto trunk button, its workspaces with Open, its bookmarks, and its changes, and clicking a change shows its description and changed files as selectable text. Filter with Ctrl+F, move with the arrow keys, press Return to show the selection in its workspace window, and use the context menu to rebase, abandon, copy ids, or open, reveal, forget, or delete a workspace.
- The workspace picker's Refresh button is replaced by Overview; the toolbar Refresh stays.

### Fixes

- Show ancestors keeps following a change after it is rewritten, for example once you resolve its conflict.
