JayJay 0.3.23 brings six betas’ worth of improvements to the stable channel.

- **Remote repositories**: work on macOS, Linux, and Windows hosts over SSH, with verified helpers and the same graph and diff tools.
- **Resolve divergence**: keep one version or choose description, contents, author, and parents, then converge in one undoable operation.
- **Make it yours**: editable keyboard shortcuts, full-window light and dark themes, and Flock keepsakes for sponsors.
- **Review faster**: mark folders together, review any mutable change, and preview SVG, SARIF, notebooks, and archive listings.
- **History and integrations**: undo/redo shortcuts, ancestor/descendant merges, Gerrit upload and update, and CLI commands for importing PRs and managing sibling workspaces.
- **Linux and Windows**: divergence resolution, shortcut editing, SSH repositories, and native Windows ARM64 packages, with fixes for Scoop launches and background refreshes.
